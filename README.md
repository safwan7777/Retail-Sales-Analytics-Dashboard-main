# Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

An interactive **Excel Sales Analysis Dashboard** designed to provide a clear view of sales performance across revenue, orders, products, customers, regions, and sales channels.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project transforms raw sales data into an interactive dashboard using **Microsoft Excel**. The dashboard enables users to explore sales performance through dynamic filters and visualizations across different business dimensions.

The analysis focuses on:

- Revenue and order performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Regional performance
- Product category analysis
- Sales channel performance
- Customer type analysis
- Top-performing products
- Quantity and order value analysis

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify trends in monthly revenue.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Analyze revenue across product categories.
- Evaluate sales channel contribution.
- Compare new and returning customer activity.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Support data-driven sales decisions.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Revenue performance varies across months, with noticeable peaks during specific periods.
- Regional analysis highlights differences in sales contribution across markets.
- Computers contribute the largest share of revenue among the product categories.
- Online and Corporate Sales are significant contributors to overall revenue.
- The dashboard highlights the highest-performing products based on revenue.
- Customer type analysis provides visibility into new and returning customer activity.
